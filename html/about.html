Origins and Inspiration

MMC is the result of years of dedication to mathematics education and Olympiad training. The idea traces back to 2017 when Hamza Ba-Mohammed, alongside his friend Mohammed JAMAL, founded EMPIMO, a mathematical training network for Math Olympiads.

EMPIMO became a hub for several impactful initiatives, including:
  • Olympic Maths Forum – A discussion platform for mathematical problem-solving.
  • Combinatorial Junior Olympiad – A competition dedicated to young mathematics enthusiasts.
  • Olympic Mathematical Junior Camp – A training camp for Olympiad participants.
  • Numerical Library – A collection of valuable mathematical resources.
  • High School Mathematics Clubs – The establishment of mathematics clubs in various Moroccan high schools.

Through EMPIMO, we identified several challenges in Morocco's approach to mathematics, spanning education, scientific research, and public engagement.

The Transition to MMC

In 2020, EMPIMO gradually became inactive and eventually dissolved in 2021 following Hamza Ba-Mohammed's resignation from its Directive Office due to various challenges. Initially determined not to return, he later reconsidered after discussions with former members, friends, and influential figures from the EMPIMO community. This led to the creation of MMC—a renewed vision for mathematics education and research in Morocco.

By the end of 2021, Hamza Ba-Mohammed gathered a team of eight passionate mathematics enthusiasts - Ayoub Fahame, Hajar El Idrissi, Youssra Ghninou, Aya Kourdou, Amine Sahraoui, Hamza Tazi, Hanae Kolone, Bouchra Sahri. Together, they worked on structuring the new association, from defining its mission to organizing the General Constitutive Assembly on March 20, 2022.

Our Mission

MMC is built on the experience and vision of its founders, with a clear mission:

  • Enhancing Mathematics Education – Providing high-quality training and resources.
  • Promoting Scientific Research – Encouraging mathematical research and innovation.
  • Engaging the Community – Creating a strong network of mathematics enthusiasts, students, and professionals.

MMC aspires to bridge the gaps in mathematics education and research in Morocco, fostering a dynamic and inspiring environment for future generations.
