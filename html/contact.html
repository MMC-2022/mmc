Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Moroccan Mathematical Community (MMC)?
MMC is a nonprofit association and NGO founded in 2022 in Rabat. We unite mathematicians, educators, and enthusiasts to promote math education, research, and cultural integration in Morocco.
What are MMC's main goals?
  • Democratize access to mathematics for all socio-economic groups.
  • Enhance education, support scientific research, and connect math with Moroccan culture.
  • Foster innovation, creativity, and sustainable human development.
Who governs MMC?
MMC is led by a National Bureau (elected every 3 years) and operates through:
  • General Assembly: Annual strategic meetings.
  • Committees: Permanent (Media, PR, Digital, Logistics) and project-based (Education, Research).
How can I join MMC?
  • Membership: Open to students, researchers, and math enthusiasts. Apply via our website.
  • Volunteer: Assist with events, content creation, or committees.
  • Donate: Support projects through financial contributions or sponsorships.
